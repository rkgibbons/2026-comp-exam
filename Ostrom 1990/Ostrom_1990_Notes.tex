\section{General Notes}
%--------------------------------------------------

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=4pt]
    \item Tragedy of the commons occurs without supporting institutions; when CPRs are properly managed by means of institutional development, the tragedy can be avoided. Leviathan solution is not optimal for this use case
    \item Best solution to the commons is the establishment of self-organized and self-governed institutions -- those who are subject to the resource and its usage should create them to manage
    \item Communication, monitoring, customization, and enforcement are all necessary traits of collective action solutions
    \item Institutional construction is a costly endeavor, creating an additional potential collective action problem -- easier to let someone else take the initiative of institutional design
    \item Potentially actually most appropriate as a lens of institutional endogeneity rather than as a substantive application?
\end{itemize}
