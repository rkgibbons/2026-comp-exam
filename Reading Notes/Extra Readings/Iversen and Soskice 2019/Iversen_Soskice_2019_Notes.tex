\section{General Notes}
%--------------------------------------------------

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=4pt]
    \item ``As we see it, the advanced capitalist democracies, for all their instability and social problems not least at present, have been remarkably resilient and effective over this whole period. What we want to understand in the book is what mechanisms have driven that resilience and effectiveness over this long century.'' Creating a framework of capitalism within advanced economies; the core analyzed outcome is democratic resilience.
    \item Paints an opposing system to prominent claims that market capitalism has ``beat'' democracy, instead saying that democracy has evolved to encompass markets but been almost strengthened through it (VERY similar to the Levy 2006 argument)
    \item ``In our framework theory advanced capitalism is driven by the advanced democratic nation-state: democracy drives advanced capitalism. And in this process the autonomy of the advanced nation-state has increased even as globalization and mutual dependence have risen.'' Three elements of the reinforcing relationship between democracy and capitalism:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item The role of government is central to facilitating effective capitalism and maximally free markets... ``Capitalism left to itself will hardly choose to operate in a competitive environment.''
        \item Parties to be electable have a reputation for managing advanced capitalism effectively
        \item Advanced capital is geographically embedded in the advanced nation-state rather than footloose (considering human and information capital rather than raw resources, for example)
    \end{enumerate}
    \item New cleavage between knowledge economy and low-productivity labor markets, yet claims it to not be a fundamental democratic threat -- largely because of the maintained prominence of the middle class and the consistent need for parties to secure its vote
    \item Democracy creates the conditions necessary for advanced markets to thrive
    \item Central issue regards the presence/nature of the median voter: are they within the middle class or the lower class? If in the middle class, then democracy and capitalism become self-sustaining and symbiotic, as the median voter is likely to support capitalistic endeavors. (Can also be fueled by the \emph{expectation} that one's children will have the opportunity to benefit through capitalistic endeavors)
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Further assumes that this is generally the case in advanced democracies: ``In our model a central component is that the large skilled workforces of the advanced sectors of the economy, and the aspirational voters who seek to join these workforces, have interests aligned with the promotion and success of advanced capitalist sectors, and are generally decisive voters.''
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
