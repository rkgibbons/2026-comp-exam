\section{General Notes}
%--------------------------------------------------

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=4pt]
    \item Democracy is a redistributive activity and therefore stands to prevail when the impacts of this redistribution can be mitigated. Two pathways through which this can occur:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Economic equality: there is little change to be expected from ``fair'' redistribution, so establishing a redistributive institution is unlikely to be particularly disruptive
        \item Capital mobility: the friction to redistribution is low/lessened such that the redistribution can occur without broad societal upheaval
    \end{enumerate}
    \item If capital is significantly mobile such that it could be redirected abroad in the face of threats/abuse, then the democratic state ultimately becomes limited in how much it can tax capital. This avoids conflict/tension and therefore supports peaceful democracy
    \item Claims that a demobilized lower class is critical for the peaceful sustenance of authoritarianism
    \item Primary operationalization of capital immobility is land: land cannot be hidden nor moved. Economic modernization involves a shift from immobility (land, natural resources) to mobility (human capital, other mobile capital)
\end{itemize}
