\section{General Notes}
%--------------------------------------------------

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=4pt]
    \item While literature has previously focused on collective peasant actions en masse, argues that the driving factor of political development is decentralized, lower-level everyday resistance (also particularly targeting indirect resistance)
    \item Everyday resistance can include intentional inefficiency, destruction of property, false compliance, feigned ignorance, foot-dragging... all construct a degree of protection against exploitative bodies
    \item Everyday resistance is rationally viable particularly because the costs are low and there exists no meaningful collective action barrier to overcome
    \item Resistance is often symbolic in some part -- actions are heavily judged by meaning and ideological intentions
    \item As landowners and wealthier farmers gained representation/influence/wealth, common peasants (evidence here is from Sedaka in Malaysia) responded with everyday rather than organized resistance in an effort to preserve previous economic structure, rights, situation, etc.
    \item Characterizes much peasant activity as at a level below organized revolt, but instead efforts to slowly squeeze against higher classes or other influential bodies/groups
\end{itemize}
