\section{General Notes}
%--------------------------------------------------

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=4pt]
    \item Stable domination or equilibrium order is \emph{Herrschaft}, which requires a claim to legitimacy. Three possible such claims:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Traditional authority -- norms, cultural rules, dynasties, etc.
        \item Charismatic authority -- devotion to a specific leader
        \item Legal-rational authority -- legitimacy vested in institutions or rules
    \end{enumerate}
    \item Charismatic authority is extra-institutional and is largely unstable; it cannot survive for an extended period of time and instead must either adapt or die. This adaptation is routinization -- charismatic authority must instead be shifted into embedded social and political structures, transforming charistmatic authority into either traditional or legal-rational authority
\end{itemize}
